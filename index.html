1. Introduction

Abundance Logistics Ltd. is committed to protecting your personal information. This Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data when you use our website or services.

2. Information We Collect

Identity data (name, company), contact data (email, phone, address), transaction data (shipments, quotes), technical data (IP, browser), and marketing preferences.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and manage the logistics services you request
  • To process quotations and contracts
  • To communicate about shipments and enquiries
  • To send newsletters where consented
  • To comply with URA and legal requirements

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your data. Shared only with carriers and port agents as needed, government bodies when legally required, and service providers under confidentiality agreements.

We implement appropriate technical measures to protect your data. Shipment records retained for seven (7) years per Ugandan commercial law.
